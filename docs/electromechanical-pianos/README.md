# Electric Pianos physical-model roadmap

**Build the Hohner Clavinet D6 first.** It adds a genuinely different action to Electric Pianos, and the first audible prototype can be checked three ways. The inventor describes the tangent and anvil, Hohner supplies a component schematic, and independent researchers measured a D6 and published real note examples. Start with three strings and two separately observable pickups. Predict the change when the player switches pickups or releases a key before expanding to the full keyboard. [D6 implementation plan](tangent-strings.md) · [D6 measurements and physical model](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1687-6180-2013-103) · [Recorded D6 examples](https://research.spa.aalto.fi/publications/papers/icmc11-clavinet/)

In parallel, **the Yamaha CP-70B is the shortest route to a verified extension of an existing engine**, and **the Hohner Pianet N is the first plucked reed to pursue**. The Yamaha has a service tuning table, per-model parts and electrical adjustment targets. The Pianet has separate revision drawings and a factory three-output level check. Each can be developed offline without waiting for the other. [Yamaha grand plan](yamaha-grands.md) · [Plucked-reed plan](plucked-reeds.md)

## Playable implementations

The Hohner models in Electric Pianos include Clavinet D6 and E7, Pianets C, L, N and T, both Cembalet circuits, Electra-Piano and the Pianet/Clavinet Duo. The D6 listening bench pairs recorded attacks, pickup positions and key releases with the modeled jack. The Duo provides both actions from one key and both recording outputs alongside its mixed jack. The Electric Grand family includes the original CP-70 and the CP-70B alongside the CP-80. [Clavinet D6](../../clavinet-d6/README.md) · [Clavinet E7](../../clavinet-d6/research/e7/README.md) · [Early Pianets](../../pianet-n/research/pianet-cl.md) · [Pianet N and T](../../pianet-n/README.md) · [Cembalets](../../pianet-n/research/cembalet.md) · [Electra-Piano](../../electra-piano/research/live.md) · [Pianet/Clavinet Duo](../../hohner-duo/README.md) · [CP-70](../../cp80/research/cp70/README.md) · [CP-70B](../../cp80/research/cp70b/README.md)

The additional family menus include the Roadmaster 64 and Baldwin classroom 88, the Storytone and Neo-Bechstein, the Claviset 200 and Selmer Pianotron, Columbia EP-61C, Vivi-Tone C 1004, Valente, Rhodes MK8, Vintage Vibe 64 and Vibanet. The instrument panel shows the selected action, responds to played notes, and provides its own controls. The optional **Sustain Mod** on the four Pianets and Duo keeps a released pad clear of the reed until pedal-up. The 28 playable selections include the passive Vintage Vibe 64 and the serviced StereoVibe Rev 4.1. Their common harp produces identical passive reference captures. The active model adds the three documented input configurations and stereo optical tremolo. On an independent 5.52 Hz passage, the recorded third modulation harmonic was −17.53 dBc and the active model gave −17.04 dBc. The six Stage 73 eras are settings within one instrument, and the MK8 effects board is an option on the MK8. MIDI 0–127 extension, pickup regulation, variable pad-return noise and the Pianet sustain latch are player controls. ### Research priorities

The ranking weighs documentation of the production action and circuit, availability of an independent comparison, and how closely the existing DSP matches the mechanism. A schematic can put a circuit at the head of the queue while the corresponding keyboard still awaits a reference instrument.

| Order | Target | First deliverable and reason for its place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Clavinet D6** | Three-key tangent model, two pickup taps and a loaded switchable preamp. Check free, clamped and released string motion against recordings and test the pickup combinations against the drawing. [Tangent strings](tangent-strings.md) |
| 2 | **Yamaha CP-70B**, using **Electric Grand 80 / CP-80** as the control | Play both string-construction boundaries on the 73-key scale. Independently reproduce the 500 Hz board trim and hold the existing 88-key renders as the regression reference. [Yamaha grands](yamaha-grands.md) |
| 3 | **Hohner Pianet N** | One early adhesive-pad action, the matching capacitive bank and board, and the three factory output taps. This unlocks the C and L without assuming their electronics match. [Plucked reeds](plucked-reeds.md) |
| 4 | **Hohner Electra-Piano** | Three asymmetric forks, seven coil groups and a circuit deck. The electrical soft pedal and mechanical sustain give two independent tests on a held note. [Forks and reeds](struck-metal.md) |
| 5 | **Hohner Pianet T** and **Cembalet** | The T has an owner-recorded dry reference and documents the magnetic branch needed by the Duo. The early Cembalet adds a true plectrum, independent damper and documented RF readout. Treat the later Cembalet N electronics separately. [Plucked reeds](plucked-reeds.md) |
| 6 | **Clavinet E7**, **Pianet C/L**, then **Clavinet/Pianet Duo** | Reuse the measured actions and verify each model's board and controls. Tackle the Duo after the string and late magnetic reed mechanisms work separately. Its split and layer outputs test their relative timing and levels. [Tangent strings](tangent-strings.md) · [Plucked reeds](plucked-reeds.md) |
| 7 | **Original Yamaha CP-70** | Its 1976 mechanics can start with the 73-key parts data. The original pickup and TR board have their own part numbers. Secure its schematic or a serial-matched trace and capture before validating the original output. [Yamaha grands](yamaha-grands.md) |
| 8 | **RCA/Story & Clark Storytone** and **Neo-Bechstein** | Start their circuits in parallel with the pilots if an electronics contributor is available. The Storytone has a period service schematic and magnetic voicing procedure. The Siemens ELV413 has a traced circuit and measured response. Keyboard, pedal and speaker comparisons need a matched specimen. [Historical instruments](uprights-and-early-grands.md) |
| 9 | **Rhodes MK8** and **Vintage Vibe Tine Piano** | Prefer the MK8 dry pickup send and the original passive Vintage Vibe 64 Classic as measurable first targets. Capture their own hammer zones, geometry and output loads before reusing Stage 73 mechanics. Model the MK8 preamp/optional FX and active Vintage Vibe configurations as separate stages. [Modern fork plans](struck-metal.md) |
| 10 | **Helpinstill Roadmaster** and **Baldwin Electro-Piano** | Survey an original Roadmaster sensor installation and its load-bearing acoustic board. On the Baldwin, confirm the transducer and rail on a specific 64- or 88-key unit. The resulting string support and pickup observations drive different solvers. [Uprights](uprights-and-early-grands.md) |
| 11 | **Vintage Vibe Vibanet**, **Columbia Elepian** and **Valente Electric Piano** | The Vibanet needs gel-release and auto-wah measurements. Identify the EP-61C pole before applying the Columbia patent. Capture the Valente's counter-hammer, reed travel and passive panel. A documented unit with dry access moves its target up this list. [Vibanet](tangent-strings.md) · [Elepian and Valente](struck-metal.md) |
| 12 | **Weltmeister Claviset / Selmer Pianotron** and **Vivi-Tone Clavier** | Trace the 200's pickup and distinguish it from the documented magnetic Selmer. For Vivi-Tone, observe one slow key cycle to resolve impact versus deflection and release. These checks determine the excitation or readout equation before implementation begins. [Claviset and Pianotron](plucked-reeds.md) · [Vivi-Tone](struck-metal.md) |

## First implementation handoff

| Work package | What to deliver at the first review |
| --- | --- |
| D6 action and pickup | A repeatable offline sequence with one wound, one middle and one treble string. Supply key travel, both raw pickup voltages, the loaded output and fixed-gain A/B audio. Include an early release and a same-key restrike. |
| Yamaha extension | A CP-70B key table traceable to model columns, two construction-boundary pairs and a circuit deck with the factory gain test. Show CP-80 baseline equivalence alongside the new 73-key output. |
| Pianet action and circuit | A serial/board and pad-material record, a video or displacement trace of pull-off and return, and the three output levels on the same triad. Demonstrate how a partially reseated pad changes a rapid repeat. |
| Shared verification and integration | Agree on units, capture metadata and holdouts with the three builders. Share the offline event sequence and measurement output first. Extract DSP when two families have proved the same operation. Integrate the first passing engine into the current Electric Pianos wrapper and retain old session recall. [Code boundaries, acceptance and packaging](shared-modeling.md) |

The first review ends with a short dry performance, numerical residuals at named signal taps, and a list of parameters still estimated. Expand to a full keyboard once the pilot predicts a reserved key or playing condition. If the pickup is known only from a patent or the only audio includes a cabinet, limiter or sampler processing, use the specific acquisition step in the family plan before claiming production-model fidelity.
